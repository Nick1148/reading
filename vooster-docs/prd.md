## 1. Introduction

The Company Reading Club Meeting Management Webpage aims to facilitate communication, record memories, and attract new members to the reading club. The target users are existing members (30-40s) and potential new members. The main features include meeting schedule announcements, photo and review bulletin boards, new member registration, meeting-specific albums, book-related quizzes, and like/comment functionality on posts. The primary goals are to increase member participation, promote communication, and foster a sense of belonging.

## 2. Goals

*   Increase member participation in reading club activities.
*   Facilitate communication and interaction among members.
*   Attract new members to the reading club.
*   Preserve memories and experiences from reading club meetings.

## 3. Target Audience

*   Existing reading club members (30-40s).
*   Potential new members interested in joining the club.

## 4. Features

### 4.1. Core Features

*   **Meeting Schedule Announcement:** Announce upcoming meeting dates, times, and locations.
*   **Photo and Review Bulletin Board:** Share photos and reviews from past meetings.
*   **New Member Registration:** Allow new members to sign up and introduce themselves.

### 4.2. Additional Features

*   **Meeting-Specific Albums:** Organize photos by meeting for easy browsing.
*   **Book-Related Quizzes:** Engage members with fun quizzes related to the books they read.
*   **Like/Comment Functionality:** Allow members to like and comment on posts to encourage interaction.

## 5. Use Cases

*   Existing members check the meeting schedule and sign up for meetings.
*   Members share photos and reviews from past meetings.
*   New members register and learn about the reading club.
*   Members participate in book-related quizzes and discussions.

## 6. Release Criteria

*   All core features are fully implemented and tested.
*   The webpage is responsive and accessible on different devices.
*   The webpage is user-friendly and easy to navigate.

## 7. Future Considerations

*   Implement a member directory with profiles and contact information.
*   Add a book recommendation engine based on member preferences.
*   Integrate with social media platforms to promote the reading club.

## 8. MVP Scope

The initial MVP release will include the core features: meeting schedule announcement, photo and review bulletin board, and new member registration. Additional features will be added in subsequent releases based on user feedback and priorities.
